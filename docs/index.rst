How to Understand and Access Your AncestryDNA Results
==========================================================

AncestryDNA is a powerful tool for discovering your family history and ethnic origins through genetic testing.

Once you have registered your DNA kit and provided your saliva sample, the next step is understanding how to access and interpret your results. Unlike traditional mail-based reports, AncestryDNA results are delivered entirely online through your Ancestry account . This guide explains the process of locating your DNA results, understanding the various components of your report, and troubleshooting common issues that may prevent you from viewing your genetic information.

Understanding Your AncestryDNA Account
======================================

The first step in accessing your DNA results is ensuring you have the correct Ancestry account. Your DNA results are tied directly to the account you used to register your DNA kit . If you have multiple Ancestry accounts or have shared an email address with someone else, you may encounter difficulties locating your results .

Each adult who takes an AncestryDNA test must register their kit on their own account . This ensures that each individual has control over their own genetic data and can manage their privacy settings independently . If someone else purchased the kit for you, they may have sent you an invitation to register it; you will need to create your own account or sign in to an existing one to complete the registration process .

If you have multiple DNA tests associated with your account, such as results for different family members, you can switch between them using the test switcher feature . This is particularly useful for parents managing tests for minor children or individuals acting on behalf of incapacitated adults with power of attorney .

Locating Your AncestryDNA Results
=================================

Your AncestryDNA results are not mailed to you; they are available exclusively online through your Ancestry account . Once your sample has been processed, you can find your results by signing in to your account and navigating to the DNA section .

From a web browser, sign in to Ancestry and click the DNA tab at the top of the page, then select Origins . If your results are ready and you are signed in to the correct account, you will see a page displaying your name and three main sections: Regions, Journeys, and By parent .

From the Ancestry mobile app, open the app, sign in, and tap the DNA icon in the bottom right corner . Once your results are ready, you will have access to the same information as the web version, including your origins, matches, and traits .

If your kit is still being processed, you will see a progress bar indicating its current status . Processing times can vary; it may take up to five weeks for Ancestry to receive your sample and another six weeks for your results to be ready after they mark it as arrived . It is normal for DNA samples mailed together to be processed at different times .

Understanding Your DNA Results
==============================

Once you access your results, you will encounter several key sections that provide different insights into your genetic heritage. Each section offers a unique perspective on your ancestry and personal characteristics.

The Origins section shows the regions around the world where your DNA is connected and where your ancestors likely lived thousands of years ago . This is the core ethnic estimate that provides a high-level overview of your genetic background. AncestryDNA uses reference populations from around the world to compare your DNA and determine your ancestral origins.

The Journeys section reveals where your relatives might have lived or moved in the past 300 years . This feature provides more recent historical context, showing the migration patterns of your ancestors. It connects your DNA to specific geographical areas and historical events that may have influenced your family's movements.

The By parent section shows what you inherited from each parent, even if they have not taken AncestryDNA tests . This feature uses advanced algorithms to separate your DNA into maternal and paternal contributions, giving you insights into which side of your family specific traits or origins come from.

Additional Features for AncestryDNA Members
===========================================

Your AncestryDNA results also include several powerful features for exploring your genetic connections and personal traits. Access to some of these features may require an Ancestry family history membership .

The Matches tab displays other AncestryDNA members who share DNA with you . These are your genetic relatives, and the list is based solely on your DNA, not on family trees . To see full family trees, common ancestors, or use tools like ThruLines, you will need an Ancestry family history membership . Shared matches, which help identify how you are related to multiple people, also require a membership .

The Traits tab shows personal characteristics influenced by your DNA, the environment, or both . These can include physical traits like eye color, as well as behavioral traits such as risk-taking or athletic ability . Traits provide a fascinating look at how your genetics may influence various aspects of your life.

If you have been invited to view someone else's DNA results, you must accept the invitation through your Ancestry account . You will receive an email invitation; clicking Review Invitation and signing in will allow you to accept access . You do not need a membership to view the ancestral origins, DNA matches, and some traits of tests you are invited to .

Troubleshooting Common Issues
=============================

Users may encounter various issues when trying to locate or access their AncestryDNA results. Understanding these common problems and their solutions helps resolve them quickly.

If you see an ad instead of your results, you might be signed in to the wrong account . Sign out and sign in with a different email or username to switch accounts. If you cannot remember your password, you can reset it . If you need help identifying any other accounts you may have, you can contact Ancestry support .

If you receive a "You are not authorized to access this page" error after clicking an email link, you are likely signed in to the wrong account . Sign out and sign in to the correct account, then click the DNA tab and select Your DNA Results Summary to view your results .

If your DNA homepage shows options to purchase or register a DNA kit instead of your results, you may not have registered your kit yet . Ensure you have used the registration code that came with your DNA kit. If you no longer have the code, you can contact Ancestry support for assistance .

If you have previously viewed your results but cannot find them now, you may have multiple Ancestry accounts . Try signing out and signing back in with a different email or username. If you still cannot find your results, contact Ancestry support .

Conclusion
==========

Accessing and understanding your AncestryDNA results is a journey that begins with proper account setup and registration. By ensuring you are signed in to the correct account and have registered your kit, you can access a wealth of information about your ethnic origins, genetic relatives, and personal traits. The various sections of your DNA report provide layered insights into your heritage, from ancient regional connections to recent family migrations and inherited characteristics.

By following the guidance in this comprehensive guide, you can navigate the AncestryDNA experience with confidence. From understanding the different tabs and features to troubleshooting common access issues, the knowledge provided here equips you to fully explore your genetic heritage. Successful access to your results is the key to unlocking your family history and discovering the stories embedded in your DNA.

Ultimately, your AncestryDNA results are a personal and powerful tool for understanding your identity and connections to the past. By taking control of your account, understanding your results, and exploring the available features, you can make the most of your genetic testing experience and uncover the rich tapestry of your ancestry.
